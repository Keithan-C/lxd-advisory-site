E-learning

Learning that works on the learner’s terms.

E-learning should reduce friction, not create it. I create structured learning experiences that are easy to follow, easy to revisit, and easy to use in real life.

What I build

  • Module-based learning journeys with clear user flow
  • Short-form, scenario-driven content for practical understanding
  • Interactive learning experiences that support recall and decision-making
  • Content designed for mobile, desktop, and repeat access
Practical

Content designed for the moment someone needs to apply a decision or process.

The result

Good e-learning saves time while helping audiences understand what matters most. It works best when it is structured, human, and built around action rather than just information.
