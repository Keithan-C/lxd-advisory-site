AI literacy

AI understanding without the hype.

The goal is simple: help people understand what AI can do, where it can be useful, and where human judgement still matters most.

What people learn

  • How AI tools work in practical, non-technical language
  • Where AI adds value and where it can create risk
  • How to evaluate outputs and ask better questions
  • How to use AI responsibly in business and education settings
Human first

AI literacy is not about replacing judgement. It is about strengthening it.

Best suited to

Teams and organizations that want to use AI thoughtfully without rushing into tools that do not fit their work, values or risk profile.

It works particularly well as a foundation for broader digital adoption, governance and change management.
