PostCheck

Understand the letter. Know what to do next.

PostCheck is a practical product designed to make official letters and communications easier to understand and easier to act on.

Why it matters

Many people receive important letters and do not know what they mean, what matters, or what the next step should be.

  • Clear explanation of key points
  • Plain-language summary of what matters most
  • Guidance on next actions, deadlines and implications
  • Support for people who need clarity without jargon
Clarity

It turns complexity into a straightforward next step for the person reading it.
