## About

LXD Advisory combines learning experience design, corporate training, and practical digital solutions.

The website brings together two main service areas:

- **Learning** — corporate learning, e-learning, instructional design, and AI literacy & compliance
- **Digital Solutions** — websites, web apps, forms, booking systems, automation, and AI solutions

LXD Advisory also develops its own digital products, including **PostCheck**.

## Design Direction

The website uses an editorial, modern, warm visual style rather than a traditional corporate or SaaS aesthetic.

### Visual principles

- Warm ivory backgrounds
- Near-black typography
- Restrained olive accents
- Large editorial typography
- Generous whitespace
- Asymmetric / broken-grid layouts
- Minimal cards and unnecessary UI elements
- Clear hierarchy
- Strong mobile experience

### Typography

**Headings:** DM Serif Display / Cormorant Garamond  
**Body:** DM Sans / Inter

## Core Idea
